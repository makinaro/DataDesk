## Round 1 report (summary)

- **[Critical] The row counter could be bypassed by shape:** only DataFrames counted rows, so a dict of rows, a CSV string, several frames or paging through `get_output` sent hundreds of rows.
- **[Major] Architecture:** the controller was called per-analysis (P11 made it per conversation); output shaping split across the untrusted kernel, the context builder and main; reports written by `datadesk-mcp` had no interception point; the plan guard sat in the relay; the partial report created a second artifact writer.
- **[Major] Decisions:** Q1's 1M-row threshold vs D-031; Q5 narrowed FR-19 (no transcript); P11 Q-G2 and P10 Q-B6 had no builder; spend unstated; the maximum overshoot P12 relies on unstated.
- **[Major] Recommendations:** Q1 cache keys and lineage from the declared name, not the parsed `select`; unseeded sampling. Q2 missed `default_rng()` and DuckDB `random()`. Q4 let revisions rewrite direction and target after results. Q5 left Continue's budgets and split undefined and pulled replay forward. Q8 missed stop reasons. Q10 let the scorer read agent-typed values.
- **[Major] Operability:** concurrent Continue could create two journal writers.
- **[Major] Measurability:** EC14-1 gameable by marking everything unverified; PA-02 vacuous at 0/0; journal entry types unspecified (raw text, resolved model ids); PA-09 missing.
- **[Major] Safety:** provenance laundering through plan constants and through the state card; literal and monkeypatch checks not built; new security-critical paths outside CODEOWNERS.
- **[Major] Statistics:** p-hacking through plan revisions; test reuse across Continue; nobody owned the split at `save_plan`.
- **Minor/Nit:** correlation-id fallback; unverified facts; electron-vite entry; checker re-run on reopen; snapshot cap enforcement; deliverable rows; turns budget; undefined terms; session count; stale text; question order.
