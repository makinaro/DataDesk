## Round 1 report (summary)

- **[Critical] Architecture:** five of six ablation switches had no owner and could only be added after the freeze, which restarts it; EC22-3's "simplified away" changes the frozen build.
- **[Major] Decisions:** `--confirm` contradicts D-034 and P12-07; per-task holdout commits contradict P12-05; 10 judge items contradict D-038's 30.
- **[Major] Buildability:** spend ignored reservations (a pass is exactly $15, over with double judging) and void headroom.
- **[Major] Measurability:** M-13, M-14 and M-05 had no measurement design; the scorer wasn't frozen and the report was hand-written; Q6 mis-classified M-14 and left M-16..M-19 and PA metrics out; tiny per-metric denominators; injection fixtures missing from the holdout.
- **[Major] Safety:** ablation switches in the product; re-runs on a seen holdout; holdout disclosure through the report; post-hoc scorer changes; a movable unsigned tag.
- **[Major] Statistics:** no decision rule; ablations without a control, confounded by provider, with optional stopping; "fix and re-run" reuses the test set; 10 recheck items can't support M-17.
- **Minor:** superseded P12 Q7 cited; unverified SDK facts; tree check blocks its own second run; run order; holdout-c design and order; P17/P18 holdout instances; M-11 per build; undefined terms; template gaps; session count.
