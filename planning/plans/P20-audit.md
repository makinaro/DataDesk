## Round 1 report (summary)

- **[Major] Architecture:** two owners for dataset quirks (lessons and notes) and for weights (notes and `studyDesign`); a pull retrieval tool beside the builder push; self-reported `lessons_used` driving confidence.
- **[Major] Decisions:** auto-applied lessons rendered from agent text (D-037 says structured fields only); text-similarity merging and promotion (P10 Q26 says structural keys); EC20-2 gated though roadmap §7.1 makes M-14 report-only; kernel findings and unchecked corrections as lesson sources (principle 7, P10 Q24); the holdout-a purge missing.
- **[Major] Operability:** no abort rule for the learning sequence; `lessons.jsonl` update semantics, concurrency and schema versioning undefined.
- **[Major] Measurability:** "apply" undefined in EC20-4 and its fixture holdout-only; M-13 gameable and underspecified.
- **[Major] Safety:** persistent injection through reflection and text merges; notes laundering into weights; lesson and note text outside D-033's counters.
- **[Major] Statistics:** no counterfactual for M-13; M-14 possibly vacuous or contaminated.
- **[Major] Completeness:** mapping table, structured fields, purge, custody, IPC, schema versions, phrasing role and parity, paths, tests, spend.
- **Minor/Nit:** stale-note re-validation owner, Q10 wording, holdout naming, ranking rule, SDK injection point, phrasing call, crash re-derivation, deleted analyses, risk ids, undefined terms, coverage of lesson classes.
