## Round 1 report (summary)

- **[Critical] `second_opinion` egress:** an optional cross-provider critic sends the review pack (answers, samples, column names) to a provider outside D-033's allowlist, against P11 Q-G2's no-override rule.
- **[Critical] Laundering advisory findings:** forged kernel records or probe findings in the pack get restated by the critic and counted as detections, and flow into P20's lessons.
- **[Major] Architecture:** the lead invokes the critic as a sub-agent, so it can skip it, time it or rewrite its input, and free text has no zod channel.
- **[Major] Decisions:** dismissals feeding M-03 (against D-036); the critic's causal adjudication (D-035) removed; the per-role experiment could use Opus (D-038); no spend stated.
- **[Major] Recommendations and buildability:** a budget cap where a reservation is needed; unfenced pack fields; three subject vocabularies; unverified SDK facts.
- **[Major] Operability:** no journal records or failure semantics for the critic and revise loop.
- **[Major] Measurability:** PA-08 credits restated findings; subjects unscoreable for tests and claims; gate statistics and inherited gates missing.
- **[Major] Safety:** the lead certifies its own fixes; injected text downgrades severities; agent-written limitations.
- **[Major] Statistics:** the experiment rule decides on noise and picks the gate arm; the plan-time critique runs after the plan locks.
- **[Major] Completeness:** deliverables missing (UI dismissals, IPC, renderer, parity, journal types, tests, paths).
- **Minor:** registry inventory, review-pack schema owner, "disputed" schema bump, M-09 target, `issues[]` as findings, trigger-point wording, oracle use of `run_method_checks`, dev tuning disclosure, done-when lines, terms, size.
